# random-bots

UCMenu: the character page's Random slot (`UC2Arena_Character`, the "?" in the middle of the grid) and the bots of
bot-variants (`BotVariants.Bots`). On the Random slot the pad's X steps through those bots; while the slot shows Random,
its icon shows two of them beside the "?".

- `UCMenu.diff`, `UC2Arena_Character`:
  - The cycle: `RandomBotMask`, a bit per entry of `BotVariants.Bots`, the bots the Back switch can choose on their own
    slots (the bot unlocked, `BotAvailable`, and the slot's own character the player's), made with `UpdateBotMask`. X on
    the Random slot (`XClicked`, `StepRandomBot`) shows the next of them in the order of `BotVariants.Bots`: Random,
    Corrosion, Korig, Mirage, Hyena, Nepthys, Ra, Memphis, Darius (those in the mask), Random again. `ValidXControl`
    lets X through there while the mask has a bot; with the handicap panel up it is refused with the error sound, as on
    any slot. The keyboard's X and a click on the hint reach it as the pad's X does (`[GUI.EmulatedJoypad]`, the
    engine's hint clicks).
  - The state: `RandomBot`, the bot the slot shows, or -1 for Random; a state of the slot, as bot-variants'
    `BotSlotMask` is: both players see it, it stays while the cursors move, and the page starts with Random. A bot that
    leaves the mask (another profile) shows Random again (`RandomShown`). Players three and four keep their own controls:
    X does nothing for them and their confirm on the Random slot is a random pick (players34).
  - The hint: the X hint (`btn_AltChar`) on the Random slot names what X shows next, the next bot, or Random while the
    last one is shown (`UpdateRandomHint`). It is there while the mask has a bot and the player's handicap panel is
    down (`UpdateControlVisibility`, `RandomHintAvailable`); another player's X hint stays inert to the mouse
    (menu-mouse, bot-variants).
  - What a bot on the Random slot shows is what the slot of that bot shows for it: its icon (`SlotIcon`), its name over
    the preview, the preview's static (`UpdatePreview`), the stats panel with its weapons and combos and the health and
    agility bars of its own slot (`UpdatePlayerStats`).
  - The choice: a confirm on the Random slot while it shows a bot (`CharacterSelected`, `ChooseRandomBot`) moves the
    player's cursor to the bot's own slot and switches that slot to its bot (`ToggleBot`), as a random pick moves it to
    the character it picks; the rest is bot-variants' choice: the bot's class in the player's URL as `BotClass`, the
    profile keeps the slot's own character, nothing is saved. A confirm while it shows Random is Epic's random pick, never
    a bot.
  - The icon: while the slot shows Random and the mask has a bot, `DrawRandomIcon` (the Random button's `OnRender`,
    after the button has drawn the "?" icon) draws the first two bots of the cycle (the one on both sides when there is
    one) over the icon's inner area to the left and right of the "?": the inside of the icon's frame, as columns of the
    bots' own icons taken 12 texels nearer their middle, so that each side shows part of a face, with a line of the
    frame's bright ring at each split, in the icon's colour and alpha (the page's fades). The bots' icons are those of bot-variants' slots (the grey Corrosion
    while the slot is not under a cursor). The geometry is in texels of the 64-texel icon (`RandomInner`). No other
    place that shows the Random icon changes, and no texture is added.
  - Log (DevUI): `random-bots: player <n> switched the Random slot to the bot <Name>` / `to Random`, and
    `random-bots: player <n> chose the Random slot's bot <Name> (slot <i>)`, before bot-variants' `chose the bot` line.
- **Not saved**: as bot-variants, nothing writes the Random slot's state or the bot to the profile or an ini.
- **Ini keys**: none. **Depends on**: bot-variants (its diff is made on that set's text).
- Generated by `tools/dev/uc-patches-regen.sh`.
