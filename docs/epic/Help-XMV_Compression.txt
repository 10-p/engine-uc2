XMV Doc
------------------------------------------------------------

VIDEO ONLY-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

c:\wm8\wm8eutil.exe -input (1)u:\xxx.avi -output (2)U:\xxx.wmv -v_mode 2 -v_bitrate 14000000 -v_buffer 1000 -v_quality 100 -v_keydist 10

xmvtool -v (3)U:\xxx.wmv -o (4)U:\xxx.xmv

pause

(1): This is the source avi file, uncompressed with audio
(2): the WMV util compesses to window media player file
(3): the WMV - for XMV compression
(4): the output'ed XMV file.

other settings:

-v_mode 2 -v_bitrate 13000000 -v_buffer 2000 -v_quality 60 -v_keydist 10 (really low quality)
-v_mode 2 -v_bitrate 13000000 -v_buffer 2000 -v_quality 94 -v_keydist 10
-v_mode 3 -v_bitrate 1200000 -v_buffer 2000 -v_quality 95 -v_keydist 10 (bit rate determines size, must be below 1.3Mbps to run real time)



VIDEO + AUDIO(Compressed)-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------


c:\wm8\wm8eutil.exe -input (1)u:\xxx.avi -output (2)U:\xxx.wmv -v_mode 2 -v_bitrate 14000000 -v_buffer 1000 -v_quality 100 -v_keydist 10

xmvtool -ic (3)U:\xxx.wmv -o (4)U:\xxx.xmv

pause

(1): This is the source avi file, uncompressed with audio
(2): the WMV util compesses to window media player file
(3): the WMV - for XMV compression
(4): the output'ed XMV file.


VIDEO + AUDIO(UnCompressed)-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------


c:\wm8\wm8eutil.exe -input (1)c:\mov\xxx.avi -output (2)c:\mov\xxx.wmv -v_mode 2 -v_bitrate 13000000 -v_buffer 2000 -v_quality 100 -v_keydist 10

xmvtool -a (3)c:\mov\xxx.avi -v (4)c:\mov\xxx.wmv -o (5)c:\mov\xxx.xmv

pause


(1): uncompressed avi
(2): WMV export
(3): uncompressed avi, with audio - Nothing else is read from this, just uncompressed audio. 
     can be a plain avi, maybe wav or mp3 (havent tried yet).
(4): WMV reference
(5): XMV output, with audio

the files will be much larger with this method, because the WMV util is not touching 
the audio (yay).
